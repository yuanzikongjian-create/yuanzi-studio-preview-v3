Collaborate

The Right-Handed God is finished. But the people in it don't have real faces yet, and no one has truly seen that valley.

If you're also wondering what this story could become, let's talk first.

What we can talk about

  1. Short films and trailers: bring one scene or one character to the screen first.
  2. Comics and visual adaptation: draw the four clans, the stone dome and the sea of grass into a world you can turn the pages of.
  3. Publishing and distribution: help the trilogy reach the right readers in the right form.
  4. Character likeness: let one of these characters wear your face.
  5. Merchandise and licensing: for later. For now, the door is open.
  6. Festivals and pitch events: take the work to more people in the right places.

Character likeness

Deep in the rainforest there is an unfamiliar world. Its people have their own faiths and desires, and people they can't bear to lose. They have already lived once on the page; now we'd like them to step into pictures.

You won't need to come in for a shoot. With your consent, we use your appearance as a reference to create a character image with AI. We start with a single image, and within an agreed range of trials and adjustments, we see together who she turns out to be.

How your photos are handled, where the image is used, for how long, and how it is shared and credited: we agree all of that clearly before anything begins. Liking the story is never a reason to agree to anything you're not comfortable with.

What we don't do

  • No volume production work unrelated to the studio's own IPs.
  • No sale of, or promises about, rights that are not yet settled.
  • No commitment to fixed timelines.

Before we begin

  • Book One stays free to read.
  • Character and scene images are AI-assisted.
  • Any paid use, physical product or filmed adaptation needs a separate written agreement.

FAQ

Do I have to pay to read the novel?

Book One is free to read on the author's blog. Books Two and Three are finished but not yet published.

Are the images on this site made with AI?

The novel's text is the author's own. Character and scene images are AI-assisted.

Can I use the character images?

Please don't use them commercially without written permission. If you'd like to use them, write to us; any arrangement will be agreed separately in writing.

Do I need to attend a photo shoot for a likeness collaboration?

No. With your consent, we work from reference images you provide. How the photos are handled, where the image appears and for how long are all agreed before we begin.

Is there an English edition?

Coming soon

How soon will you reply?

Usually within a week.
