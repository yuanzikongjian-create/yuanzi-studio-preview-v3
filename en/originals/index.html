Synopsis

How many times can one man die?

Deep in the Borneo rainforest, under a vast dome of rock, lies the Dark Valley, where sunlight enters only from the south. Its four clans have each carried civilisation deep into the body and the mind. The Yaonan share one mind: their chief, Ailu, commands his army through a web of thought and feels the death of every soldier who falls. The Shuoying read the world through spatial computation and electromagnetic sense, so their chief, blind from birth, can still perceive the person in front of her. The Lage grow up bonded to spirit beasts, "Two Hearts, One Soul", and to lose the beast may be to lose half of oneself. The Tesi hold precise command of their bodies and can read the feelings and intentions of others.

They have gifts outsiders would kill for, and still they cannot stop killing one another.

Professor Alistair Puller, an archaeologist, comes into the valley with his former student Yan Ming and the pilot Ximen, chasing a question his late father left behind: is God right-handed?

Change arrives before any answer. Yan Ming suddenly understands an unknown language and loses his own home dialect; the world turns into angles and probabilities before his eyes, and his feelings are stripped away layer by layer. Ximen's wounds close and his strength surges, but that body is burning through his life. Ze'en, king of the Lage, drives the four clans towards a single day of battle as his children drift away from him, one by one. Wildflower, an exiled Tesi divine general, wants people to understand one another; the other road asks them first to bow to the blade.

When gunfire from the outside world finally breaks into the valley, some grow stronger, some see more clearly, and some begin to lose the people they used to be.

Characters

  • Wildflower

    An exiled Tesi divine general who once saved thousands on a burning rope bridge. She can feel what others feel, but she cannot choose for them.

  • Yan Ming

    A young man from Kuching, Puller's former student, with a gift for ancient scripts. In the valley he comes to understand a strange language, and slowly loses his own.

  • Alistair Puller

    An archaeology professor who walks into the rainforest to answer his late father's question: is God right-handed?

  • Ximen

    A 39-year-old Iban former air-force transport pilot. In the valley he tastes recognition for the first time, and begins to pay for his strength.

  • Ze'en

    King of the Lage. He once hung from a cliff to save his dying children; now he means to end the clans' division with a war.

  • Ailu

    Chief of the Yaonan. He commands his army through a web of thought, and dies a little with every soldier who falls.

  • Lanji

    Chief of the Shuoying, blind from birth, who knows the world through space and electromagnetic sense.

Look and feel

Mist, cold light beneath a dome of stone, a river on a rainy night, seas of grass and of forest. Ink blue, charcoal, fog blue, silver and moss green, with now and then a touch of dark gold. Not a bright jungle adventure, but a quiet, deep world that makes you want to take one more step inside.
