Studio

About the studio

Yuanzi Studio is the personal creative studio of Yuanzi, based in Kuching, Sarawak.

Yuanzi, whose English name is Horace Yuan, is a writer from Sarawak. He has published a science-fiction novel, 《慧军创世纪》 (unofficial English title: The Legend of Borneo), and made the short film Valentine, which opened the Kota Kinabalu International Film Festival (KKIFF).

Then he began a story set in the rainforests of Borneo, and it grew into three books. The Right-Handed God trilogy is the studio's first original IP, and all three books are finished.

The studio is small, and it only works on stories it believes in. We'd like to work with people who know this land and know how to tell a story, and imagine together what else the story could become.

The novel's text is the author's own. Character and scene images are AI-assisted.

Mission

To finish the stories that grew out of Borneo, to tell them well, and to find each of them the form that will carry it to readers and audiences further away.

Vision

When the world reads Borneo, we want it to find more than a rainforest: people who think, love, misunderstand one another, and still try to understand, there under the canopy.

What the studio does

  • Original IP development: worlds, characters and visual materials, grown from long-form fiction.
  • Adaptation and co-development talks: short films, trailers, comics and other visual forms. These are directions we are open to discuss, not commitments.
  • Publishing and distribution talks: print, e-book and other channels.
  • Character likeness and licensing interest: character-likeness collaborations now; merchandise and licensing to be discussed later.
  • Industry festivals and pitch events: bringing the work to markets, labs and pitch sessions.

What we don't do

We don't take on volume production work unrelated to our own IPs, we don't sell or promise rights that are not yet settled, and we don't commit to fixed timelines.
